# Earlier wills, pets, funeral wishes and gifts to organizations

Reviewed November 16, 2026. The four subjects are separate interview steps in all 97 locations. The [location table](planning-choices-by-location.csv) gives the default form, the effect of each gift choice and a local public source for each location. “Recorded for review” describes what **this kit** can do; it is not a finding that local law prohibits a gift. A person can always finish the interview and receive a package.

## What the visitor chooses

- **Earlier document.** The first interview question covers an existing will, handwritten will, foreign will or signed change. Yes and Unsure explain that simply answering the interview changes nothing; keep the originals, and have the documents read together before signing anything new. The visitor can continue or correct the answer. Having a previous will alone does not remove the normal will or form. The review and completion screens, Start Here, signing guide and answer record carry the professional-review warning. Leave this service instruction off the will and handwriting reference. The handwriting reference still tells the maker to copy it and never sign the printout. Original publishers’ documents retain their text. The professional-review warning is a product precaution, not a claim that employing a professional is universally mandatory. See [Government of Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/life-events/death/estates-wills.html) and [UK government guidance on new wills and codicils](https://www.gov.uk/make-will/updating-your-will).
- **Pets.** Visitors can identify their pets, first and backup carers, and what someone should do on the first day. They can choose practical instructions, transfer all companion animals they own outright to a willing and capable surviving carer, or describe another arrangement. The practical sheet can be shared immediately; no trust, care fund or obligation to accept is created. An express transfer goes into the authored instrument on the 25 supported routes: Canada, Australia, New Zealand, England, Wales and Northern Ireland. If the first choice cannot take the pets, the transfer goes to the eligible backup if named; if neither takes them, they stay in the estate. A bespoke plan, requested care funding, missing main carer or unsupported transfer is preserved for a professional. Sources: [Ontario SPCA](https://ontariospca.ca/blog/planning-for-your-pet-in-your-will/), [Québec Civil Code, art. 898.1](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cs/ccq-1991), [NSW Trustee and Guardian](https://www.nsw.gov.au/family-and-relationships/planning-for-end-of-life/learn-about-wills), [RSPCA legacy guidance](https://www.rspca.org.uk/getinvolved/donate/legacy/faqs), [SPCA New Zealand](https://www.spca.nz/donate/leave-a-gift-in-your-will/pet-legacy-plan). A charity care scheme requires its own arrangements.
- **Funeral or remains.** No preference, burial, cremation and other arrangements each have a direct choice; an optional description records the visitor’s own words. It goes in a separate, shareable PDF. The recipient should have it now. Some laws give effect to wishes in specified circumstances; a generic preferences sheet is not presented as a statutory instrument, permission to dispose of remains, or consent to donation. Compare [Québec Civil Code, art. 42](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cs/ccq-1991), [British Columbia, ss. 5–6](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04035_01) and [NSW funeral wishes](https://www.nsw.gov.au/family-and-relationships/planning-for-end-of-life/funeral-wishes).
- **Organizations.** A repeatable step accepts a specified sum in local currency or one owned keepsake. Each entry has its own organization, asset or amount, and review decision. On supported authored routes the visitor confirms the body’s correct legal name, capacity to accept the gift, and unrestricted purpose. No individual-survivorship or family-substitution wording is applied to a body. A project restriction, uncertain identity or requested alternative fallback goes to review; local law can affect what happens to a failed gift. No tax status is promised, and an informal club, local branch or charity registration is not necessarily a separate legal entity. [CRA charity-name search](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/charities-giving/list-charities/search-tips-charities-listings.html) · [Canadian not-for-profit corporations, s. 16](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-7.75/section-16.html) · [Québec Civil Code, art. 618](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cs/ccq-1991) · [UK gifts of money or items](https://www.gov.uk/donating-to-charity/leaving-gifts-to-charity-in-your-will) · [Australian charity structures](https://www.acnc.gov.au/for-charities/start-charity/you-start-charity/legal-structure) · [Law Society of Ireland on gifts to unincorporated bodies](https://www.lawsociety.ie/news/news/Stories/bequests-to-non-profits-and-unincorporated-bodies/) · [Singapore Commissioner of Charities](https://www.charities.gov.sg/PublishingImages/Resource-and-Training/Guides-Templates-Awards/Guides/Documents/Legalese.pdf).

## Where the organization gift goes

| California court form | 1 | Unrestricted cash to a verified charity only. Paragraph 4 shares five physical rows with other cash gifts. The donor signs each applicable box. An item, sixth gift, noncharitable body or other disposition goes to review. |
| Professional-review package | 52 | Both wishes can be fully recorded. The package identifies them for a local professional and does not issue an instrument that omits them. This includes fixed public forms, the Texas court form, routes for formal delivery and authored instruments for which this review did not establish compatible wording. |

Maine’s optional statutory form is a separate route from its authored will. Its [printed §2.3](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/18-C/title18-Csec2-517.pdf) allows three signed cash gifts to charitable organizations or institutions. The visitor enters name and amount on each line and signs it. The chat and guide explain this; the original is delivered byte-for-byte unchanged. Without the signature, or if the recipient no longer exists or declines, the gift does not take effect. The original [California form, paragraph 4](https://selfhelp.courts.ca.gov/sites/default/files/2026-02/6240-Statutory-will-form.pdf) expressly provides for a named charity. The [Texas court forms, §3.3](https://www.txcourts.gov/media/1456774/239037.pdf) use wording and survival conditions for a person. The service does not insert organization language into that clause.

For Germany and Poland, the **heir must perform the legacy**: the item or money is not treated as automatically owned by the organization at death. The additional eligibility and family checks still apply. See the [German Civil Code, §§2147 and 2174](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/englisch_bgb/englisch_bgb.html) and [Polish Civil Code, arts. 927, 968 and 972](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2023/1610/text.html). The [European e-Justice succession portal](https://e-justice.europa.eu/topics/family-matters-inheritance/inheritance/succession_en) and each location’s existing sources were used to distinguish the other routes, including the [single-heir Slovak kit](https://e-justice.europa.eu/topics/family-matters-inheritance/inheritance/succession/sk_en) and [Dutch formal delivery](https://e-justice.europa.eu/topics/family-matters-inheritance/inheritance/succession/nl_en).

The US authored routes are [Alaska](https://courts.alaska.gov/shc/probate/wills.htm), [Hawaii](https://data.capitol.hawaii.gov/hrscurrent/Vol12_Ch0501-0588/HRS0560/HRS_0560-0001-0201.htm), [Idaho](https://legislature.idaho.gov/statutesrules/idstat/title15/t15ch1/sect15-1-201/), [Iowa](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/2026/633.3.pdf), [Maine](https://legislature.maine.gov/legis/statutes/18-C/title18-Csec1-201.pdf), [Massachusetts](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartII/TitleII/Chapter190B/ArticleI/Section1-201), [Michigan](https://www.legislature.mi.gov/documents/mcl/pdf/mcl-700-1106.pdf), [Montana](https://mca.legmt.gov/bills/mca/title_0720/chapter_0010/part_0010/section_0030/0720-0010-0010-0030.html), [Nebraska](https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=30-2209), [New York](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/EPT/3-1.3), [North Dakota](https://www.legis.nd.gov/cencode/t30-1c01.pdf), [South Carolina](https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t62c001.php), [Utah](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title75/Chapter1/75-1-S201.html) and [Washington](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=1.16.080). The statutory sources include organizational recipients or corporations; they do not establish that every proposed recipient is eligible. ## Continuation, privacy and language

Every interview question has one “Leave a note for a professional” control below the answer box. Saving returns to the same question and restores a typed draft. The visitor’s answer determines the will or form. The written note stays with its question in the companion documents, with a direction to have a qualified local estates professional review the will before signing or using it. Visitors can edit a note on its question or remove it from the review screen. Choosing “Continue anyway” with a required answer unresolved produces planning notes.

Official forms keep their prescribed wording. For example, California’s statutory will directs the maker to enter information only in the spaces provided. The package carries notes and review instructions in the accompanying PDFs. [California statutory will and instructions](https://selfhelp.courts.ca.gov/sites/default/files/2026-02/6240-Statutory-will-form.pdf)

At a pause, Back returns to the original question. Continue anyway records the actual concern and optional explanation. It does not check a box on the visitor’s behalf. At review, completion and inside the ZIP, the visitor sees the matters to discuss and a direction not to sign or use the kit as a will before a qualified local estates professional reviews it with them. If known facts do not fit a will, the primary PDF is a professional-review planning document. Sponsorship at download follows the same location and campaign rules for every kind of kit. A sponsor interaction is optional; the answer and review data stay in the browser.

The 19 language editions translate the new cards, choices, legal clauses, instructions and warnings. Names, amounts and the visitor’s own wishes remain as entered. The separate meaning check reads actual PDFs using both pypdf and PDFium: it identifies the main and backup carer, survival and willingness, funeral preference, organization, amount/currency, object, the heir’s role, and the force of the prohibition on signing. The browser check also verifies the actual controls, delivery of two separate review explanations, and a fictional sponsor’s ZIP without disclosing the answers. Publisher originals keep their own language. Local legal-language certification, operator classification and acceptance of particular procedures remain open; this review does not establish public release approval.
