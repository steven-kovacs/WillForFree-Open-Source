The original November 13, 2026 publisher set was fetched again on November 15 and matched its pinned SHA-256 digests. On November 16 the Hancock County Registry of Probate edition replaced the Maine Revisor printout. The printed clauses stay intact. For California and Texas, the software writes only to existing editable fields and leaves witness, signature and notary fields for the ceremony. The entire Maine form is completed on paper.

When a visitor confirms that cash entries belong to the same recipient, California paragraph 4 and Texas section 3.3 receive the total in one gift box or row. The answer record keeps each entry. For repeated charity gifts on the blank Maine form, the signing guide lists the confirmed total to enter on the charity’s section 2.3 line.

California requires the maker to sign one box in paragraph 5. If none or several are signed, the residue goes by intestacy. Separately valid gifts under paragraphs 2–4 can still operate. A signed paragraph 9 waiver leaves the court discretion to require a bond. [Probate Code §6224](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=6224.&lawCode=PROB) · [California Rule of Court 7.201](https://courts.ca.gov/cms/rules/index/seven/rule7_201)
Texas family and marital-status branches select the court’s revised original. For the married maker with children, signing both §2.2 boxes or leaving both unsigned puts choice 1 into effect if the spouse survives by 30 days. All four forms use the will-maker, two witnesses and a notary together. [July 7, 2023 court order and revised forms](https://www.txcourts.gov/media/1456774/239037.pdf)

Hancock County publishes its six-page Maine statutory will for free printing under 18-C §2-517(2). The final PDF in the kit is the unchanged 78,532-byte county download. On page 3, §2.4B has an initial box, a 23-underscore amount line after “to my spouse” and a separate signature line. The visitor can record A, B or C in the chat or leave that decision for the printed form. The legally operative elections, entries and signatures belong on paper. The formerly distributed five-page Revisor printout omitted this writable amount line. The two editions also paginate their other gifts, appointments, witnesses and optional self-proof differently. Texas also publishes four bilingual English–Spanish editions. All four blank PDFs for the selected edition are requested as a set. The browser chooses the applicable one, fills independently mapped fields, and leaves the three execution fields for the ceremony. For a married person without children, section 2.3 has ten named alternate beneficiary spaces in English and eight in the bilingual publication. A user who has already entered nine or ten names can retain them all in the English edition or explicitly revise the list. Every edition has a pinned hash in config/official-forms.json. California and Maine supply their English originals. WillForFree translates their accompanying documents and identifies the form language in the chat.

Arizona, Colorado, Florida, Kentucky, Minnesota, North Carolina and Ohio receive the same checked-in English blank instrument from the preview link and from every language’s ZIP. The signing guide and introduction follow the selected language. Colorado’s fixed publication expressly addresses substitute descendants under §15-11-603. The right to distribute sponsored forms remains open. California’s publisher has separate terms for its materials. [California Judicial Branch terms of use](https://courts.ca.gov/about/terms-use)
