Your kit is free. Your browser puts the will together using the answers you give in this tab.

Is it actually free?

Free download. No card, email or donation.

Choose Download ZIP to get your kit. When there’s no sponsor in your area, the ZIP downloads straight away. If there’s a sponsor, its optional offer appears beside the button and in a separate file in the ZIP.

We don’t ask for donations. Paid placements can help cover our costs. Visiting a sponsor is optional. You get the same free will either way.

WillForFree charges nothing. If your local signing process involves a notary, court or other professional, they may charge for their part.

Who can see my answers?

As you type, your answers stay in this tab. Your browser builds the document and ZIP. Your names, gifts, messages and documents stay out of sponsor reports and remote AI requests.

Download your kit to keep a copy. It contains personal information, so store it somewhere private. If you close or refresh this page, your next chat starts fresh.

When does the chat use AI?

Typically, you can go from the first question to downloading your will kit without the chat ever using AI. Your browser builds the kit from your answers. AI help is on by default. Think of AI as a manager who helps keep the interview on track. If you have a question or go off course, it can point you to a help topic so you can pick up where you left off.

We never send your personal information to an AI company.

AI sees only topic names, such as executor duties or privacy, and the kind of step you’re on. Your question, names, answers and will stay in your browser.

The chat checks our written answers first. AI can suggest which help topic to read, then you can return to your interview question. If we don’t have an answer, we say so.

Use without AI

This turns AI off for this tab. You can still ask questions and download the same kit. To turn AI back on, use the switch in the chat.

What else leaves my browser?

Location and site visits

When you open the tool, GeoJS receives your IP address to suggest a country and region. You confirm the location. When your kit needs an official or blank form, the site receives a request for that location’s form. In Texas, everyone gets all four blank court forms. Your browser selects one using your answers about your family. The host and network services also receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address.

With your permission, Google Analytics uses a browser identifier to measure visits, traffic sources, interview starts, kit downloads and sponsor inquiries. Your answers and messages stay out of Analytics.

If you allow analytics, Google receives device and connection data, including your IP address, and may store cookies. You can change your choice in Analytics settings at the foot of the page.

How are AI help requests limited?

When your browser asks the site for help choosing a topic, the site uses a cookie that lasts up to 24 hours. Short-lived counters use protected codes for your browser and network to limit repeated requests. They contain no chat text or will answers. If a limit is reached, your browser can still show explanations and finish your kit.

Offers and download counts

Your browser uses your confirmed location to check for an offer. If you download a sponsored kit, it sends a campaign ID and a random, single-use receipt to count that download. A campaign ID can indicate an area. Sponsors see totals. Your name, answers and will aren’t included. If you visit an offer, the sponsor’s own site opens in a new tab. You decide what to share there.
