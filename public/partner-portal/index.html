What counts as a download?

A download counts when a visitor sees your offer, their browser starts downloading the ZIP and the reporting service receives confirmation during the campaign. A retry with the same receipt counts once. The same person can start another kit. Blocked confirmations and campaign limits can cause undercounts. Reports show estimated downloads per campaign. Dates use UTC.
