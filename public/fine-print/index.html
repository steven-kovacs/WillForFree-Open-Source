Who makes my will?

You do. You choose who receives your property. Where the form allows it, you can also name the person who will settle your estate. WillForFree supplies the form and instructions. It is a self-service tool, not a law firm. No lawyer reviews your individual will through this service.

Make a will for yourself, from your own decisions, free of pressure. If someone helps you type, the answers must still be yours. Read the whole document and make sure you understand it before signing.

Responsibilities and liability

By checking the box and downloading your kit, you agree to these terms.

Our policy is to check the official will-making sources for every region we serve each month. We use those sources to keep our kits accurate. Laws can change between reviews, and errors or omissions can occur.

Before using a will, confirm your jurisdiction. Read the whole document and check the names, gifts and appointments against your wishes. Follow the current local requirements for signing, witnessing, handwriting or official delivery that apply to your kit.

Start with the official sources linked in your kit. Independently confirm the current requirements with the relevant local authority or a qualified local legal professional before relying on the kit.

What do I do with the download?

Open 00-Start-here.pdf in your ZIP. It identifies the will or form, the local steps and the source behind them. If you chose private notes, the ZIP contains notes for a future will; there is nothing to sign.

Depending on your location, you may need to fill in a form, sign with witnesses, write the entire will in your own handwriting, or take it through a notarial or court procedure. Typing a name in the chat or signing a printed copy of a handwriting guide does not complete those steps. Keep the original as the local instructions direct.

For example, in Ontario you can print the will and sign with two witnesses, or personally handwrite the entire will and sign it. In the Netherlands, Malta and Portugal the kit requires an official procedure. In Estonia the domestic will in the kit expires after six months if you are still alive. The lookup above identifies the method for your location.

Does the kit fit my situation?

Some include choices for parents. The chat explains what the selected form can do. You can change the language of the chat and kit. Publisher forms keep their original wording. If you own a business, check what can pass through your estate and who you want to receive it. The personal kit can cover that interest with the rest of your estate. A separate business gift, a second will or instructions about running the business need to fit together before you sign. Read the guide for business owners.

Keep your existing will and any signed changes safe. This interview does not change them. Signing a new will could cancel earlier instructions, including ones made abroad. Have the documents checked together before signing.

If you have an earlier will, property abroad, family members with support rights, a dispute, or a wish the form cannot express, pause before signing. A qualified estates professional where you live can advise you. A guardian named in a will does not necessarily replace a surviving parent or determine what a court will decide.

This is a will concerning your estate after death. It does not appoint someone to make medical or financial decisions for you during your lifetime. An account that already names a beneficiary, or property owned jointly, may pass outside your will.

Can I add more gifts or personal wishes?

Where the form supports separate gifts, you can add an item or a cash amount, name its recipient, then add another until you are done. The review shows which gifts are in the will. Some forms have fixed space or offer only a single heir and a backup.

You can record gifts of money or items to organizations. The interview tells you whether the gift goes in this will or needs professional review.

Pet-care instructions, memorial preferences, digital information and messages stay in a separate file. Those notes do not change who inherits or give anyone permission to sign into an account. Keep passwords, access codes and banking details out of the chat and the will.

What is free, and who pays for it?

No card, no email gate, no donation request, no paid download. If an offer covers your confirmed area, it appears at download and in a separate file inside the ZIP. Paid offers name the sponsor. You can download without visiting the sponsor or sharing your contact details.

WillForFree does not charge to make or download a kit. An independent notary, court or professional may charge for a service or appointment. A required appointment is still required even when the kit itself is free.

Where do my answers and files go?

If you use the contact form, we send your reply address and message to info@willforfree.com. Sponsor inquiries also include your organization. Firebase holds the message until our email service passes it to our inbox. Only the fields you enter in the contact form go with it.

When you press Send, Cloudflare checks your browser for spam and forwards your message to our mail queue. Cloudflare Turnstile Privacy Addendum

Names, gifts and documents stay in this browser tab. Reloading or closing the chat clears its answers. Your downloaded kit contains PDFs with personal information and has no password. Store it somewhere private. Each new visit starts fresh, and WillForFree cannot recover a lost copy.

When the chat is available, it asks GeoJS for a location suggestion. GeoJS receives your IP address and ordinary connection details. There is no GPS request. A VPN or a trip can give the wrong result; you confirm your permanent home yourself.

Requests for a blank or official will form can identify its jurisdiction to the host. For Texas, the browser fetches the same four blank court forms for everyone and picks one locally. It sends no family answers with those requests.

The interview and kit are completed in your browser without AI. If a help question matches several topics, the site can automatically ask an external AI service to choose one of them. That service receives only the topic names and the kind of step you’re on. We never send it your messages, names, selected location, gifts or documents.

Requests to choose a help topic use a first-party anti-abuse cookie for up to 24 hours and server-side counters based on keyed codes for the browser and network. Request times count toward limits for one hour. Operators receive the category of a triggered limit. Chat text, raw IP addresses and will details are excluded from these counters and alerts.

Your browser matches sponsor offers to the location you confirmed. When a sponsored ZIP starts downloading, it sends Firebase the campaign ID and a random receipt to count it once. A regional campaign ID can indicate its service area. The receipt contains no names, will contents or location fields. Sponsors can see their own campaign totals. Receipts are eligible for deletion 30 days after the campaign ends. Hosting and network providers may process ordinary connection data.

With your permission, we count visits and will kit downloads to help improve WillForFree. Your answers and messages stay out of Google Analytics.

We give your browser a random ID. It helps us see how people find the site, which pages they use and how many start a will or ask about sponsorship.

Google receives your IP address and basic information about your device. It may save cookies in your browser. You can change your choice anytime in Analytics settings at the bottom of the page.

What if the law or my plans change?

Check the current local source before you sign, especially if you use an earlier download, move or your family changes. A later download does not update a signed original. If you edited an official PDF after downloading it, those edits are not saved in the chat. Keep your edited copy.

A will’s effect depends on the law that applies, your circumstances, and how you complete it. WillForFree cannot verify capacity, witnessing or official delivery, or guarantee what a court will decide.
