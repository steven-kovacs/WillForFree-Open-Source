Our Mission

A will for everyone who needs one.

I kept hearing how many people didn’t have a will. What if I made it so easy that anyone who needed one would actually make one? No forms to wade through, no account to create, no emails you never asked for. Just one question at a time.

Steve, founder of WillForFree

Who’s still without a will?

Adults who said they had no will, in three national surveys.

Canada
50%Angus Reid Institute · 2023
United States
67%Pew Research Center · 2025
United Kingdom
57%Co-op / Opinion Matters · 2026

In Canada, four in five adults under 35 said they had no will. Across all ages, only 37% said theirs was up to date.

A little easier to start.

In the Canadian survey, some people felt too young to need a will. Others thought they had little to leave or worried about the cost.
