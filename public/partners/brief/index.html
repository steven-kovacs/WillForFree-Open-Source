Sponsorship brief.

A market sponsorship reaches people across the area you serve. A branded partner link gives your team a free kit to share with current and prospective customers.

Market sponsorship

Choose a city, province, state or country where you do business. During your campaign, visitors in that area see your labelled offer beside their kit. That includes people who come directly to WillForFree.com, find us through search or follow a company’s branded link.

A credit union can invite people across a province to a financial planning session. A lender serving one city can invite local homeowners to compare mortgage options.

We agree on the placement, dates, creative, budget and any paid outreach with you before a campaign begins.

Branded partner link

WillForFree.com/[Your-Company]

Your link brings people to a page with your logo alongside WillForFree’s. Share it after a meeting, in a newsletter or on a sign at your branch. People see your branding when they use your link.

The kit gives you a new channel to introduce your products or services. Include a separate brochure, a guide or an invitation to book a conversation. Visitors can take the free kit and decide whether to contact you.

Choose a branded link on its own, without buying regional advertising.

What makes an offer relevant?

A parent may want to explore education savings. A homeowner may want to compare mortgage rates. Someone with a complicated estate may want to speak with a lawyer. Build your offer around the question your business answers best.

Campaign placements use the visitor’s confirmed country or region. People decide whether to contact you, and their interview answers stay private.

What your portal shows

Your portal shows estimated kit downloads for each campaign and its dates. A download counts when the browser starts it and confirms it to us. You see totals. The visitor’s name, answers and will stay private.

Tell us about your audience

  • The area you serve, who you’d like to reach, and the language they use.
  • Your preferred dates and budget, including any paid outreach you want.
  • One optional offer and the destination on your own site.
  • What success looks like for your organization and how you measure it.
